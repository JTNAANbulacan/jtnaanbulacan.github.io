Welcome, Worship Team

Your JTNAAN worship workspace brings the music team and presentation team together while keeping their live working environments separate.

About this app

One shared home for the people who lead worship and serve behind the scenes.

JTNAAN Worship Team Hub combines your existing Worship Hub and Presenter under one sign-in. Songs, setlists, chords, lyrics, presentation media and live session tools continue to use the existing shared system.

What the app can do

Purpose-built tools for each part of the worship team.

♫

Worship Hub

Shared song library, setlists, lyrics and chords, instrumentalist and vocalist views, audience monitors, tuner and live team tools.

▣

Presenter

Prepare and present song slides, Scripture, custom slides, countdowns, backgrounds and presentation controls.

⇄

Live synchronization

Keep the presenter, audience display, vocalist and instrumentalist views connected to the same live session state.

How it helps the worship team

Less switching between tools, clearer preparation and a shared live flow.

Musicians can follow the song structure, chords and lyrics. Vocalists and instrumentalists can follow the same live progress. The presentation operator can prepare slides and backgrounds in the dedicated Presenter workspace. Each team member only receives the tools assigned by the Super Admin.

Quick access

Open your assigned live workspace when the team is ready.

Installation tutorial

Keep JTNAAN Worship Team Hub easy to open

You can add this page to your device for quick access. An internet connection is required for login, shared songs and live synchronization.

  1. Windows / Desktop: Open this page in Chrome or Edge, open the browser menu, then choose Install app or Create shortcut if available.
  2. Android: Open this page in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. iPhone / iPad: Open this page in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Sign in using your existing JTNAAN Worship Team username and password.
  5. Choose Presenter or Worship Hub from Live Session. Use the Home button to return to this dashboard.
Do not clear browser site data while using a live session. Your login is shared by the two workspaces, while personal appearance preferences stay on this device.
